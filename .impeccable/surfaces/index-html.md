# Surface brief: Master Imóveis one-page site (index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience now: Master Imóveis owners judging a speculative proposal. Audience represented: people renting or buying in Greater Vitória, and owners listing a property.
Action: search (wired to Master's CRM URLs), talk on WhatsApp/phone, list a property.
Proof on hand: 30 years in the Capixaba market, CRECI 2917-J, real services, 12 real property types, 25 real neighborhoods in 6 cities, real address/phones/hours. Listings are illustrative and labeled.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis from CDN, pt-BR, full content without JS or with reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the building Master sells. Scrolling rides an elevator from the térreo to the cobertura, each floor one real category of Master's inventory, and the ride ends on Vitória at night. It refuses the category default where a hero, a search bar and a card grid are the whole story.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-hour Vitória. Night ink fields (#0b111c) alternate with travertine stone fields (#ebe5db). Master red #c72923 is rationed to what moves (the floor indicator, the active tab edge, progress, pressed states and the hand-drawn swash) and to the one primary action of each section. That exception follows the user's answer making Master red the accent. Anton sets the MASTER wordmark at architectural scale, Bodoni Moda sets display lines and floor numerals, and Albert Sans sets UI with tracked caps labels. Annotation tags with hairline leader lines are pinned to photographs, like tags on a sales-stand scale model.

STORY: The visitor recognizes Praia do Canto at dusk and reads Master's own slogan. They then search, or ride up the floors to find their type, and see example listings. They meet 30 years of Master and end in a conversation. Owners find "Anuncie" with the "parceira ideal" promise and a form that writes the WhatsApp message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed Praia do Canto blue-hour photo. "MASTER" in Anton spans about 90vw and sits behind the tower tops through an image-derived matte; on load it rises from behind the skyline. The header sits over the photo. Bottom-left: the serif slogan with a red swash under "investimento", plus one support line. Bottom-right: a search dock (Comprar/Alugar, tipo, bairro, quartos, Buscar). On the far-right rail: the photo credit and a round "Térreo" floor button that becomes the elevator counter.

FORM: This is the brief-pinned world (the Nexora and Homevia pages and the Aurelia ascent video) fused with grounded candidate 3, the sales-stand maquete with tags pinned to architecture, at position 3 on the ordered list. Seed key 842687b2. Named raises: Rietveld, red only on the moving edge. Oscilloscope, the floor rail is a measured scale with a tick per floor and numerals that step instead of floating. Quilt, every photo carries its caption tag on a visible leader line.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
The MASTER word rising from behind the real Praia do Canto skyline, then the pinned elevator ride with the floor counter stepping from Térreo to Cobertura.
